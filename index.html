Advanced HDPE Pipe Manufacturing Process

Our state-of-the-art extrusion technology ensures consistent quality, optimal material properties, and dimensional accuracy in every pipe we manufacture.

High-Grade Raw Material Selection

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity.

  • PE100 grade material
  • Optimal molecular weight distribution

Precision Extrusion Process

High-precision single-screw extruders with barrier technology ensure complete homogenization of the polymer melt at controlled temperatures.

  • Controlled melt temperature
  • Uniform polymer flow

Controlled Cooling System

Multi-stage water cooling tanks ensure gradual, uniform cooling to prevent stress and maintain dimensional integrity.

  • Multi-stage water bath cooling
  • Stress-free solidification

Precision Sizing

Calibration sleeves ensure exact outer diameter conformance to ISO and IS standards.

  • Vacuum calibration
  • Laser measurement

Quality Control

Every pipe undergoes hydrostatic pressure testing, dimensional checks, and visual inspection per IS 5984 standards.

  • Hydrostatic pressure test
  • 100% dimensional check

Marking & Traceability

Inkjet printing of all required marking: size, pressure rating, standard, batch number, and manufacturer details.

  • ISO compliant marking
  • Full batch traceability

Precision Cutting

Automatic pipe cutters ensure burr-free, square cuts at specified lengths for downstream jointing operations.

  • Automatic length cutting
  • Burr-free cut ends

Packaging & Dispatch

Pipes are bundled or coiled, end-capped, labelled, and wrapped for safe transport to site.

  • End-capped protection
  • Custom coil or straight lengths
